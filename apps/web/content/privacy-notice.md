### Who is responsible

[Technische Hochschule Ingolstadt, address]. Product owner: Prof. Dr. Ignacio Alvarez.
Data protection officer: [name, contact].

### What UXie is for

UXie helps you understand the research papers of your course through dialogue. Using it is
optional. Your conversations are **not used for grading**.

### What we process and why

- **Your account**: your university email address and password (stored by our authentication
  provider), and a random pseudonym (for example `S-3fa9c1d2e0`) that identifies you everywhere
  else in UXie. Purpose: providing the service.
- **Your conversations** with UXie, your learning progress per paper, the project description you
  choose to enter, and your feedback on replies. Purpose: providing the tutoring service and
  improving it (teaching support).
- **Usage data** such as the number of turns per day, error codes and response times, which never
  contain what you wrote. Purpose: operating the service, limits and costs.
- **Research use only with your consent.** If you tick the separate research box, pseudonymous
  copies of your conversations may be used for research on teaching. You can withdraw at any time
  on your account page; this does not affect your access to UXie. Lawful basis: [consent,
  Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR]. Without consent, your conversations are never part of research exports.

Lawful basis for the service: [e.g. Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR with the relevant state higher-education law].

### Who can see your data

- Your **instructor** can read conversations to improve teaching. They see your pseudonym, not
  your email address; revealing your identity is a separate, logged action.
- **Hosting providers** (processors with data processing agreements): Vercel (web application,
  EU region Frankfurt), Render (background processing, Frankfurt), Supabase (database and file
  storage, Frankfurt), Twilio SendGrid (sign-in and account emails; [region: EU data residency, or
  USA under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework / standard contractual clauses]).
- **AI model provider**: to answer you, the paper text and your messages are sent to the AI model
  provider configured by the instructor ([Anthropic by default; inference in the US/global
  regions]). Your name, email address and IDs are never sent. Providers whose terms allow training
  on inputs are not used.

### How long we keep it

Conversations are deleted [6 months after the end of the semester], unless you consented to
research and a documented reason to keep them exists. You can delete your account at any time.

### Your rights

You can download your data and request deletion from your account page, and you have the rights
of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and to complain to a supervisory
authority ([Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht / BayLfD]). Contact: [email].
